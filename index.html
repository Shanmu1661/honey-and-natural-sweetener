Our Harvester's Code

From Mountain Sanctuaries to Your Table

Commercial honey is frequently ultra-filtered and boiled above 160°F, killing vital pollen and live enzymes. At Amber & Oak, we practice generational gentle apiculture with zero micro-filters and zero pasteurization.

01
Sub-Alpine Foraging

Hives positioned in secluded mountain flora, miles away from synthetic pesticides and industrial monocultures.

02
Gentle Herbal Smoke

Bees are soothed with organic lavender and pine needle smoke during comb inspection, preserving hive calmness.

03
Cold Centrifuge Spin

Gravity cold-spinning extracts the pure honey at hive temperature (below 95°F) with no thermal degradation.

04
Batch Lab Certified

Every batch undergoes independent spectrometry analysis testing for moisture, floral pollen profile, and zero C4 sugars.

The Science of Living Raw Honey
