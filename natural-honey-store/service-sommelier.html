About the Program

Unlock the extraordinary aromatic complexity of unpasteurized raw honeys in your culinary program. Certified by the Italian National Register of Honey Sommeliers, our consulting team collaborates with executive chefs, pastry artisans, and beverage directors to curate transformative flavor pairings.

From dialing in exact glycemic sweetness ratios in sourdough laminations to formulating botanical shrubs, raw honey simple syrups, and cheese board pairings, we provide comprehensive gastronomic guidance and staff sensory training.

Consulting Services
Pastry & Baking Formulations
Calculated glucose-to-fructose ratios for moisture retention and crust caramelization.
Cocktail & Beverage Mixology
Cold-soluble raw honey syrups, infused bitters, and sparkling mead aperitifs.
Artisanal Cheese Pairing
Sensory flavor-mapping contrasting mineral honeydew with aged goat, pecorino, and blues.
Staff Sensory Masterclasses
Hands-on staff tasting workshops evaluating floral aromas, crystallization, and acidity.
