Origin & Botanical Sourcing

The Julian Alps of Slovenia offer isolated high-altitude meadows fed by melting limestone snowpack. The bees forage uninhibited on wild sub-alpine flora, generating a light amber nectar with unmatched enzymatic vitality.

  • Geographic Sanctuary: Julian Alps, Slovenia (1,200m Elevation)
  • Harvest Season: Late Spring / Early Summer
  • Predominant Forage: Wild Thyme, Mountain Clover, Alpine Dandelion
  • Color Grade (Pfund scale): 34mm (Light Amber)

Live Bio-Enzyme Activity

Unlike commercial processed syrups, raw unheated honey is biochemically active. Key enzymes preserved in this jar:

  • Diastase (Amylase): Converts starches into natural simple sugars. Retained at high 28 Schade units.
  • Invertase: Facilitates digestion of complex sugars into bioavailable energy.
  • Glucose Oxidase: Produces natural trace hydrogen peroxide for organic antibacterial action.
