Our Heritage

Guardians of the Ancient Hive

For four decades, Amber & Oak has championed the preservation of indigenous pollinator sanctuaries, harvesting raw single-origin nectar without heat, pasteurization, or synthetic alteration.

Since 1984

Bees First. Always.

Our founder, Henrik Thorne, established our first apiary in the Cascade foothills with three hand-built wooden Langstroth hives. While industrial agriculture began boiling honey to extend supermarket shelf life, Henrik insisted on keeping every drop cold-extracted, unpasteurized, and full of raw vital energy.

Today, our family of cooperative apiarists spans wild mountain meadows across Slovenia, the German Black Forest, the Canadian Laurentians, and the Western Himalayas. Each region produces a completely distinct terroir dictated by floral blooms, soil minerals, and seasonal rainfalls.
