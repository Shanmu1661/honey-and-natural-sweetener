Apiary Guided Tours & Raw Honey Tasting

About the Experience

Step into the sacred world of the honeybee at our Cascade foothills sanctuary. Led personally by our Master Apiarists, you will don full protective ventilated beekeeping suits, open active hive supers, observe the queen laying eggs, and taste warm honeycomb sliced directly from the wooden frame.

Following the 60-minute apiary walk, guests are welcomed into our Timber Barn Tasting Room for a sommelier-led sensory flight of six single-origin raw honeys paired with regional cheeses, toasted sourdough, and cold-pressed botanical infusions.

Frequently Asked Questions

We supply full professional ventilated beekeeper suits and calm the hives with herbal lavender smoke. Stings are exceedingly rare (less than 0.1% of tour guests over 10 years). However, if you possess a severe bee venom allergy (anaphylaxis), we advise enjoying our indoor tasting room.

Please wear closed-toe shoes (boots or sneakers) and long socks. Avoid strong synthetic perfumes, colognes, or hairsprays as strong scents can confuse the bees' delicate pheromones.

Honeybees remain inside the hive during heavy rains. In case of inclement weather, tours can be rescheduled or converted to an extended indoor honey extraction and candle making workshop.
