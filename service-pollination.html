Commercial Crop & Orchard Pollination

About the Program

Maximize fruit set, boost crop grade yields, and secure uniform fruit symmetry across your commercial acreage. Amber & Oak supplies robust, disease-free European honeybee (Apis mellifera carnica & ligustica) colonies specifically conditioned for high-efficiency early spring foraging in orchard environments.

Our apiculture team manages logistics, palletized placement along orchard windbreaks, nighttime staging to reduce bee disorientation, and telemetry-backed colony weight tracking to ensure maximum pollen retrieval throughout the bloom cycle.

Program Specifications
8–10 Frame Colony Strength
Verified brood frames with actively laying certified hygienic queens.
IoT Hive Telemetry
Live tracking of colony weight, internal temperature, and flight activity.
Turnkey Night Logistics
Direct flatbed drop-off and pickup timed strictly with bloom windows.

Frequently Asked Questions

We establish formal spray protocols with your orchard manager. If crop protection applications are required during bloom, treatments must occur after sunset when bees have ceased foraging, utilizing bee-safe chemistry.

Due to high seasonal demand across the Pacific Northwest, early booking 60–90 days prior to expected bud break is strongly recommended to guarantee hive allocations.

Our colonies are acclimatized to temperate climates and will fly in temperatures starting at 12°C (54°F). If unseasonal cold spells delay bloom, hive staging is adjusted at no additional holding fee.
