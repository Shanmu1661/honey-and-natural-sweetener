Independent Spectrometry & Purity Verification

About the Program

Protect your brand integrity and assure consumers of genuine floral authenticity. Amber & Oak’s independent analytical laboratory utilizes high-resolution isotope ratio mass spectrometry (IRMS) and nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) profiling to verify botanical origin and detect foreign sugar syrups.

We provide exhaustive testing protocols covering diastase enzyme activity (Schade units), invertase, hydroxymethylfurfural (HMF) thermal degradation markers, moisture content, and multi-residue pesticide and antibiotic screens.

Analytical Capabilities
EA/LC-IRMS Carbon Isotope
Detection of C4 cane/corn syrups and C3 rice/beet syrup adulterants down to 1%.
Melissopalynology Pollen Scan
Microscopic botanical pollen identification to verify exact geographic origin.
Diastase & Invertase Assays
Photometric verification of biologically active living enzymes (DN > 8).
Certified COA Documentation
Detailed laboratory Certificate of Analysis compliant with ISO/IEC 17025.
